How to take a beautiful picture

  1. Light first. Face a window or the sun — never put your subject against the light.
  2. Golden hour. Soft, warm photos come right after sunrise / before sunset.
  3. Rule of thirds. Keep the subject on the grid lines, not dead center.
  4. Get closer, not zoomed. Move your feet; digital zoom softens detail.
  5. Tap to focus, then drag the exposure down slightly for richer colors.
  6. Steady + clean lens. Wipe the lens, hold with two hands, breathe out, shoot.
  7. Straight horizons. Use the level bubble; keep lines parallel.
  8. Shoot 4K/8K when light is good; drop to 1080p when dark (bigger pixels, less noise).
